Frequently asked questions

Good decisions start with clear answers.

Here are the essentials for organizations and members using raay.

?Still need a hand?Talk to your organization administrator for help with access or eligibility.
What can we use raay for?

Create secure elections and polls for representative choices, committee decisions, and questions that matter to your members.

How does organization verification work?

Organizations verify a phone number, and administrators use two-step verification. This helps establish a trusted organization and admin account.

Who is allowed to vote in an election?

Organizers set the eligibility rules when configuring an election. Members who meet those rules can participate; check the election details if you are unsure about access.

Can a ballot be anonymous?

Yes. Anonymous voting is optional and the voting mode should be clear on the ballot. In anonymous mode, participation records are kept separate from ballot records.

What does the audit history show?

raay keeps a server-authoritative record of important administrative and election actions, giving organizers a history they can review.

How do I check my vote receipt?

Open Verify Receipt from the navigation and enter your receipt code to check it. The receipt helps confirm submission without displaying your selection.
