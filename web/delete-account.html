Account controls

Delete your Raay account

Use this page from any browser to reach the account-deletion flow. You do not need to reinstall the mobile app.

Request deletion online

1Sign in to the Raay web app using the same account you want to delete.
2Open Settings, then the Security tab.
3Choose Delete account, confirm, and complete the recent sign-in and enrolled second-factor check.

Continue to Raay sign in  →

Can't complete the sign-in or second-factor check? Use the developer contact shown on Raay's Google Play listing and ask for help with account deletion. Do not send your password or authenticator code.

What deletion removes

Raay deletes your authentication account, profile, organization memberships, participation records, receipts, notifications, support reports, nomination applications, and profile photo.

Records that can remain

Ballot records, frozen eligibility rosters, and administrative audit logs remain to preserve election and organizational records. In identifiable elections, ballots may remain associated with the voter, and frozen rosters or audit history may retain the account identifier. The current service does not apply a fixed automatic deletion period to those election and audit records.
