Privacy Policy

Raay · Provided by Sonryan Technologies · Effective November 1, 2026

In plain language: Raay uses account details to sign you in and operate organization voting. Anonymous ballots are stored separately from voter participation records. Account deletion is available in the app and on the Raay web app; some election and audit records remain for election integrity.

Information Raay processes

  • Account and profile: email address, sign-in provider, name, optional phone number, profile image, and account/security settings.
  • Organization activity: memberships, roles, department assignments, invitations, joining-code activity, notifications, and organization profile information.
  • Election activity: frozen eligibility rosters, participation records, ballot submissions, receipts, nominations, and election administration records. Anonymous ballot choices do not contain a voter ID in their ballot record; identifiable elections associate a ballot with its voter by design.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and technical error details may be sent to Firebase Crashlytics on supported native platforms to diagnose app failures.
  • Device preferences: appearance, language, and accessibility preferences are stored by the app for your experience.

Support and package enquiries

If you request an election package or assisted setup, Raay records your verified account email, organization name, estimated voter count, optional planned date and notes. Platform administrators use these details to review and follow up on your request. Submitting an enquiry does not collect payment or activate a license. Enquiries are removed with your support reports when you delete your account.

Why and where it is processed

Raay processes this information to authenticate accounts, operate organization workspaces, enforce voting eligibility and one-vote rules, issue and verify receipts, calculate and publish results, protect the service, and diagnose failures. The app uses Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, and Cloud Functions. These services process information on Raay's behalf under Google's Firebase terms and privacy commitments. Google Sign-In is an optional authentication method.

Ballot privacy

Election organizers choose anonymous or identifiable voting when configuring an election. Anonymous ballot choices are stored separately from voter participation records and omit the voter identity and ballot timestamp. The service still records participation to prevent repeat voting. Identifiable ballots include the voter association by design. Receipt verification confirms that a ballot record exists; it does not reveal an anonymous voter's choice.

Sharing

Raay shares data with the Firebase services listed above to provide the app. Organization owners and administrators can access organization and election information allowed by their role. Published results are visible according to the election's publication settings. Raay does not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

You can request account deletion from Settings → Security → Delete account, either in the installed app or after signing in to the Raay web app. Deletion removes your Firebase Authentication account, user profile, organization memberships, participation records, receipts, notifications, support reports, nomination applications, and profile image. Account deletion requires a recent identity check and the account's enrolled second factor.

Ballot records, frozen eligibility rosters, and administrative audit logs are retained after account deletion to preserve election and organizational records. Identifiable ballot records may continue to identify the voter as part of an identifiable election record. Frozen rosters and audit history may retain the account identifier as part of the election record. The current service does not apply a fixed automatic deletion period to those retained election/audit records; organization records remain until the applicable organization or election records are removed under the service's controls.

Security and your choices

Raay uses server-side authorization, role checks, and Firebase security rules to restrict access. No online system can promise absolute security. You may update your profile and sign-in methods in Settings. You can also contact the developer about privacy questions or requests using the contact details shown on Raay's Google Play listing.

Children

Raay is intended for organizations and their members, not specifically for children. Organization administrators are responsible for deciding who may participate and for following the laws that apply to their organization.

Policy updates

We will update this page when the service's data practices materially change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
